RATE AND METHOD OF APPORTIONMENT OF SPECIAL TAX
The special tax applicable to each Assessor’s Parcel in the City of Fillmore (“City”) Community
Facilities District No. 2 (Balden Towne Plaza Public Improvements) (the “CFD”), County of
Ventura (“County”) California, shall be levied each Fiscal Year by the City Council or its
designee, in an amount determined by the City Finance Director or his or her designee, by
applying the following procedures. All of the real property located in the CFD shall, unless
exempted by law or the provisions described herein, be taxed to the extent and in the manner
herein provided.

DEFINITIONS
Acreage or Acres means the gross acreage of any parcel of real property in the CFD, calculated
to two (2) decimal places, without any deduction for any intrusions or limitations of usage,
including, but not limited to, easements, public or private rights of way, utility installments, or
parking area improvements; but with deduction for any publicly owned real property or
easements which make impracticable the commercial use of the property. The Acreage of each
Parcel shall be as determined by the City Engineer (or designee thereof) (the "Director"). The
Director may rely upon a certification or certifications of one or more licensed civil engineers (or
such other documentation as the Director deems appropriate in the circumstances) in making
the determination as to the Acreage of any specific Parcel.
Administrative Expenses means any and all reasonable costs of the City incurred in
connection with the establishment or administration of the CFD including, but not limited to,
Fiscal Agent fees and expenses, costs of calculation, levy and collection of the Special Tax,
arbitrage rebate calculation expenses and any rebatable amount, and an allocable portion of City
staff expense and administrative overhead related to the CFD.
Annual Building Tax Rate is equal to the Annual Tax on Buildings divided by the Total
Building Floor Area.
Annual Special Tax is the total dollar amount required to meet the obligations of the CFD in
a Fiscal Year, determined under the Calculation of Annual Special Tax section below.
Annual Land Tax Rate is equal to the lesser of the Annual Tax On Land divided by the total
of all Acres in the CFD, or the Maximum Land Tax Per Acre.

Annual Parcel Special Tax for any Parcel is equal to the Annual Building Tax Rate multiplied
times the sum of Floor Area Of Building for all buildings on such Parcel, plus the Annual Land
Tax Rate multiplied times the Acreage of such Parcel.
Annual Tax on Buildings is the lesser of i) 87 percent of the Annual Special Tax or ii) the
Maximum Building Tax Per Square Foot multiplied times the Total Building Floor Area.
Annual Tax On Land is equal to the Annual Special Tax minus the Annual Tax On Buildings.
Bonds means any bonds (including any refunding bonds) issued by the City under the Fiscal
Agent Agreement, the principal of and premium, if any, and interest on which are payable from
or secured by a pledge of the Special Taxes.
CFD Boundary Map means the map showing the external geographic boundaries of the CFD
as approved by the City Council of the City, acting as the legislative body of the CFD, and as
amended from time to time.
Contributions means tax increment revenues paid by the Fillmore Redevelopment Agency to
the City under a Reimbursement Agreement for public improvements within the Central City
Redevelopment Project relating to the Bonds by and between the Agency and the City, which
tax revenues have been remitted to the Fiscal Agent during the Fiscal Year prior to the Fiscal
Year for which the Annual Special Tax is being calculated.
Fiscal Agent Agreement means any and all agreements (and any supplements thereto)
between the City and a fiscal agent or agents, providing the terms of the Bonds, including the
principal and interest payable on the Bonds.
Fiscal Year means the period starting on July 1 and ending on the following June 30.
Floor Area Of Building is the area included within the surrounding exterior walls of a
building or portion thereof, exclusive of i) vent shafts, ii) courts, and iii) non-retail uses in
buildings of more than 10,000 square feet which buildings are used for retail purposes (e.g.,
mezzanine floors in such retail buildings), all as shown on or determined by reference to City
building permits. The floor area of a building, or portion thereof, not provided with
surrounding exterior walls is the usable area under the horizontal projection of the roof or floor
above and includes each floor of multi-story buildings, with the exception of any non-retail use
(such as use of a mezzanine floor) in a building of more than 10,000 square feet which building
is used for retail purposes. For purposes of this definition, "court" is a space, open and
unobstructed to the sky, located at or above grade level on a lot and bounded on three or more
sides by walls of a building. Floor Area of Building shall be determined by the City Engineer (or
his or her designee) by reference to City-approved building plans or such other documentation
as the City Engineer shall determine. The Floor Area of Building of all buildings in the CFD is
determined on or about May 1 of each year.
Maximum Building Tax Per Square Foot is $0.60 per building square foot per year,
through Fiscal Year 1995-1996; and for each Fiscal Year thereafter the Maximum Building Tax

CALCULATION OF ANNUAL SPECIAL TAX
The City Finance Director (or his or her designee or agent) shall determine the Annual Special
Tax to be levied in the CFD during each Fiscal Year, in accordance with the following steps.
County Assessor’s information and City building permit information collected up and until May
1 of the Fiscal Year shall be used to determine the Annual Special Tax for the next Fiscal Year.
Step 1:

Determine the sum of scheduled debt service, including principal, interest and
sinking fund payments if any, on the Bonds for the calendar year commencing in
such Fiscal Year (the “Debt Service”).

Step 2:

Subtract an amount equal to the sum of (i) capitalized interest available to pay such
Debt Service, and (ii) the earnings for such Fiscal Year on any fund established under
the Fiscal Agent Agreement, the earnings on which must be used to pay Debt
Service, based upon the then-current amount of such earnings on deposit therein,
(iii) any amounts of Contributions held under the Fiscal Agent Agreement as of the
beginning of such Fiscal Year and available for credit against the Annual Special Tax
as specified therein, and (iv) any other monies held in the special tax fund for the
Bonds available for payment of Debt Service.

Step 3:

Add the estimated cost of Administrative Expenses for the CFD for such Fiscal
Year.

Step 4:

Add an amount equal to the delinquency in the collection of Special Taxes levied in
the prior Fiscal Year, subject to applicable law, and any amount required to provide
for corrections that were determined to be applicable during the Appeals and
Interpretation Procedure in Section VI.

Step 5:

The sum of Step 1 through Step 4 yields the Annual Special Tax for the Fiscal Year.

APPORTIONMENT OF THE ANNUAL SPECIAL TAX
The Annual Special Tax determined per the Calculation of Annual Special Tax section shall be
apportioned annually to the Parcels within the CFD as follows:
Step 1:

Calculate the Total Building Floor Area.

Step 2:

Calculate the Annual Tax On Buildings by determining the lessor of i) 87% of the
Annual Special Tax or ii) the Total Building Floor Area multiplied by the Maximum
Building Tax Per Square Foot.

.
Step 3:

Apply the Annual Building Tax Rate to each Parcel in the CFD.

Step 4:

Calculate the Annual Tax on Land by subtracting the result of Step 2 from the
Annual Special Tax determined in the Calculation of Annual Special Tax section.

Step 5:

Calculate the Annual Land Tax Rate for each Parcel by determining the lesser of i)
the Maximum Land Tax Per Acre, or ii) the amount calculated by dividing the
Annual Tax On Land by the Acreage of the CFD.

Step 6:

Calculate the Annual Parcel Special Tax for each Parcel. The result will be the sum of
Steps 3 and 5.

PREPAYMENT OF THE SPECIAL TAX
The lien of the Special Tax on any Parcel of real property shall be released upon payment of the
Prepayment Amount, as defined below, and the resulting redemption of a portion of the Bonds.
Prepayments may be made on any date after which all of the Bonds have been issued. Only
prepayments resulting in a release of the Special Tax lien with respect to a Parcel shall be
allowed. Partial prepayments to reduce, but not eliminate, the Special Tax lien shall not be
permitted.
The Prepayment Amount for any Parcel required in order to remove the lien of the Special Tax
shall be the greater of the results of the following two formulae:
Formula 1:
Step 1:

Divide the sum of Floor Area Of Building for all Parcels to be prepaid by Total
Building Floor Area of all Parcels in the CFD.

Step 2:

Multiply the result of Step 1 by the outstanding principal of the Bonds. Round this
amount up to the nearest multiple of $5,000.

Step 3:

Multiply the result of Step 2 by the premium, if any, due on the Bonds on the next
date for which notice of redemption can be given, determined by reference to the
Fiscal Agent Agreement.

Step 4:

Add the amount of interest that will accrue on the principal amount of Bonds to be
redeemed (such principal amount being the result of Step 2) from the last interest
payment date for the Bonds prior to such prepayment to the next available date of
redemption of the Bonds, determined by reference to the Fiscal Agent Agreement.

Step 5:

Add to the result of Step 4, the total amount of all delinquent and current Special Tax
levies levied on the Parcels to be prepaid, but not yet collected.

Step 6:

Add to the result of Step 5 the estimated cost of City staff and consultant fees and
expenses, and Fiscal Agent fees and expenses required to effectuate the prepayment
of the Special Tax and the resulting redemption of Bonds.

Step 7:

Multiply the quotient derived in Step 1 by the Reserve Requirement, as defined in the
Fiscal Agent Agreement. Subtract this amount from the result of Step 6.

Formula 2:
Step 1:

Divide Acreage for all Parcels to be prepaid by total Acreage of the Parcels within the
CFD.

Step 2:

Multiply the result of Step 1 by the outstanding principal of the Bonds. Round this
amount up to the nearest multiple of $5,000.

Step 3:

Multiply the result of Step 2 by the premium, if any, due on the Bonds on the next
date for which notice of redemption can be given, determined by reference to the
Fiscal Agent Agreement.

Step 4:

Add the amount of interest that will accrue on the principal amount of Bonds to be
redeemed (such principal amount being the result of Step 2) from the last interest
payment date for the Bonds prior to such prepayment to the next available date of
redemption of the Bonds, determined by reference to the Fiscal Agent Agreement.

Step 5:

Add to the result of Step 4, the total amount of all delinquent and current Special Tax
levies levied on the Parcels to be prepaid, but not yet collected.

Step 6:

Add to the result of Step 5 the estimated cost of City staff and consultant fees and
expenses, and Fiscal Agent fees and expenses required to effectuate the prepayment
of the Special Tax and the resulting redemption of Bonds.

Step 7:

Multiply the quotient derived in Step 1 by the Reserve Requirement, as defined in the
Fiscal Agent Agreement. Subtract this amount from the result of Step 6.

APPEALS AND INTERPRETATION PROCEDURE
Any property owner who feels that the portion of the Special Tax levied on their property is in
error may file an appeal no later than March 1 of the Fiscal Year in which the levy occurred, with
the City Finance Director, or his/her designee, appealing the levy of the Special Tax on his or
her property. The Finance Director, or his or her designee, will promptly review the appeal, and
if necessary, meet with the applicant, and decide the merits of the appeal. If the findings of the
Finance Director or his/her designee verify that the Special Tax levied should be modified, the
Special Tax levy for future Fiscal Years shall be corrected and a credit against future Special
Taxes shall be arranged, if applicable. Any over charges shall be corrected solely by means of
adjustments to future Special Tax levies; no cash refunds shall be made. The decision of the
Finance Director or his/her designee shall be final.
The City Council may, by resolution, clarify any vagueness or uncertainty as it relates to the
Special Tax rate, the method of apportionment, or any definition(s) applicable to the Rate and
Method of Apportionment of the Special Taxes.

EXEMPT ACREAGE
Notwithstanding any other provision of this Rate and Method of Apportionment of Special
Taxes, the following area within the CFD (the “UNOCAL Parcel”) shall not be apportioned any
of the Annual Special Tax and shall be wholly exempt from the levy of special taxes. The
Acreage of the UNOCAL Parcel shall not be included within the Acres of the CFD for purposes
of computing the Annual Land Tax Rate, and any buildings on the UNOCAL Parcel shall not be
included within the Floor Area of Building for purposes of computing the Total Building Floor
Area. It is hereby acknowledged that the UNOCAL Parcel will be removed from the boundaries
of the CFD following the recordation of a parcel map in the Ventura County Assessor’s Office
which designates the UNOCAL Parcel as a separate Parcel. The legal description of the
UNOCAL Parcel is as follows:

County of Ventura, State of California, as per map thereof recorded in the Office of the
